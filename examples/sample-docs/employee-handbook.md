# Employee handbook

## Working hours

Standard hours are 38 per week. Roastery and warehouse staff work shifts published two weeks ahead. Office staff may work from home up to two days a week after probation, agreed with their manager.

## Leave

Full time staff receive 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Leave requests go through the HR portal and need approval two weeks ahead, except for emergencies. Sick leave: tell your manager before your shift starts; a medical certificate is needed after three consecutive days.

## Equipment

New hires get a laptop on their first day. Laptops are managed devices; do not install software outside the company catalogue without asking IT. Report a lost or stolen device to IT immediately so it can be wiped remotely.

## Leaving

The notice period is one month for staff and three months for managers. Return your laptop, badge and keys on your last day.
