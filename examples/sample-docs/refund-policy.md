# Refund and returns policy

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days of the parcel reaching our warehouse.

Roasted coffee is perishable. Opened bags cannot be returned, but if the coffee arrived stale or damaged we will replace it or refund it without asking for the bag back.

## Late deliveries

If an order arrives more than seven days after the promised delivery date, the customer may request a full refund of the shipping charges. If it arrives more than fourteen days late, the customer may refuse the parcel and receive a full refund including shipping.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in and open the order.
2. Choose the items and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any collection point.
