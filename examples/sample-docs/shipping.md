# Shipping service levels

Domestic orders ship within two business days of roasting. We roast on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, so an order placed on a Saturday is roasted on Monday and leaves the roastery by Wednesday.

Standard delivery takes two to four business days. Express delivery is next business day when the order is placed before 11:00. Express is not available for orders above 12 kg.

International orders may take up to three weeks. Customs charges are the responsibility of the recipient.

Tracking numbers are emailed once the parcel leaves the warehouse. Cold brew concentrate ships with an ice pack between May and September and never on a Friday.
