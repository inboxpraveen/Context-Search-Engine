Roastery safety manual, chapter 1

Everyone on the roastery floor wears closed shoes, hearing protection near the roaster and a hair net in the packing area. Visitors sign the log at the front desk and are accompanied at all times.

Fire safety: the chaff collector must be emptied at the end of every roasting session. Chaff is the most common cause of roaster fires. The fire blanket and the CO2 extinguisher hang next to the roaster control panel. Never use water on a roaster fire; close the drum air, cut the gas and smother the drum.

Burns: the drum, the cooling tray and the exhaust pipes stay hot for at least an hour after the last batch. Use the heat resistant gloves in the drawer under the control panel.

Lifting: green coffee arrives in 60 kg bags. Do not lift a full bag alone; use the pallet jack or ask a second person.
